## Locked script

1. Before making any sight adjustments, understand what this target is telling you.
2. Always make the center crosshairs your aim point when zeroing your weapon.
3. Each small square measures how far your shots are landing from the center crosshairs.
4. On this target, each square in the columns represents one front-sight up-or-down click. Each square in the rows represents three rear-windage clicks, right or left.
5. Fire three to five shots before making an adjustment.
6. The center of your shot pattern gives a better picture of where your shots are landing.
7. Once you have a shot pattern, Tap-n-Score can measure where it is in relation to the center crosshairs and tell you what adjustment to make.
8. Next, we'll learn the sights.
