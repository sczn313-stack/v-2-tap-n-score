## Universality direction locked

**Universal means one authoritative implementation inherited by every applicable Smart Target—not separate implementations made to look alike.**

The Universality Pass will audit and consolidate, at minimum:

- SEC shell and structure
- typography and visual hierarchy
- accordion architecture and behavior
- Session Details
- firearm, equipment, and ammunition presentation
- Session 2 and history capability
- mobile behavior
- navigation
- Vault and reopen behavior
- shared actions and controls
- scoring presentation patterns where missions legitimately share them

Target-specific authority remains target-specific: identity, geometry, mission rules, scoring parameters or engines where genuinely different, correction logic, and specialized analysis.

## Inheritance test

> If a universal component must be changed independently in four target implementations, it is not universal.

## Target Five boundary

IDPA will be the first Smart Target born under the new hierarchy. IDPA implementation may begin only after:

1. the universal architecture is Founder-approved; and
2. the existing targets demonstrate inheritance from that architecture.
