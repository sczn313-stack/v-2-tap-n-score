# M/FAM Runtime Authority

Date: 2026-09-08
Status: lean runtime contract governed

## Founder lock

> **AUTHORITY = AUTHORITY.**
>
> **We recognize the printer's target, establish its photographed geometry, and score it.**

SCZN3 does not independently re-authorize an authoritative printer or issuer
statement. It records the exact publisher, product, face, representation,
specifications, and provenance.

M/FAM uses one shared zeroing capability. A family is reusable knowledge, not a
second approval committee.

## Executable chain

```text
PRINTER AUTHORITY
  exact publisher -> product -> face -> printed specifications
        |
        v
RECOGNITION
  which exact target/representation is in the photograph?
        |
        v
UGO
  source photograph -> governed physical geometry
  + TOP / BOTTOM / LEFT / RIGHT orientation
        |
        v
EQUIPMENT AUTHORITY
  exact active sight configuration -> control -> click value -> direction
        |
        v
BACKEND
  aim + observations + geometry + mechanics -> governed result
        |
        v
TAP-N-SCORE
  result -> adjustment -> confirmation -> SEC -> Ballistic Vault
```

Each surviving responsibility has one necessary job:

| Responsibility | Necessary question |
|---|---|
| Printer Authority | What did this printer or issuer make and publish? |
| Recognition | Which exact product, face, and representation is this? |
| UGO | What do photographed coordinates mean physically, and is orientation proven? |
| Equipment Authority | Which sight controls and mechanical increments apply? |
| Backend | What correction follows from the governed evidence? |
| Tap-n-Score | How does the shooter receive, confirm, and preserve that result? |

## Family reuse

A governed family stores facts shared by qualifying faces:

- physical geometry;
- aim relationship;
- measurement authority;
- zeroing procedure;
- applicable mechanical branches;
- confirmation authority.

An exact target binding supplies the differences:

- printer/product/face identity;
- exact representation;
- recognition/registration package;
- family reference;
- applicable equipment/mechanical reference.

Presence of that explicit binding in the governed runtime registry is the
Backend authorization decision. There is no additional family-conformance vote,
admission disposition, classification vocabulary, scale-proof counter, or
authorization-sweep counter in the executable path.

The family record does not overwrite identity. Baker remains Baker, Speedwell
remains Speedwell, Army remains Army, and their exact faces remain independently
recognizable through SEC and Ballistic Vault.

## What was retired from runtime

The following were useful during discovery but had become redundant runtime
bureaucracy:

- the separate six-gate Family Conformance/Admission registry;
- pairwise target-classification vocabulary;
- per-face admission dispositions;
- authorization-sweep accounting;
- scale-proof accounting;
- root-gap coverage accounting;
- duplicate `GOVERNED`/`runtimeStatus` votes on records already inside the
  governed authority registry;
- the duplicate M/FAM binding for the protected modern `m4_25m_zero` reference,
  whose original authority already owns it.

Their historical evidence and reports remain preserved in Git and the
authority-evidence history. They do not execute and do not get another vote.

```text
      -> 35 exact product-face representations
        -> 23 cataloged geometry records/candidates
          -> 14 procedure records/buckets

```

The current M/FAM runtime registry contains:

- 22 exact executable physical-face bindings;
- 3 governed reusable family authority packages;
- 1 universal zeroing road;
- 0 product-specific zeroing implementations.

The protected modern `m4_25m_zero` reference still executes through its original
target authority. It is no longer counted twice as an M/FAM family binding.

Known faces without a runtime binding remain knowledge. Recognition reports the
exact face and its genuinely missing facts. It does not manufacture another
classification hierarchy or tell the shooter to provide “more target” unless
the actual registration evidence shows a coverage failure.

## Fail-closed boundaries that remain

Simplification does not weaken truth:

1. Recognition must identify the exact printer product and face.
2. UGO registration must map the source photograph to the governed geometry.
3. Registration must prove TOP, BOTTOM, LEFT, and RIGHT before directional
   correction can be released.
4. Source-photo evidence and the projected governed result must reconcile.
5. Manufactured physical differences that can change the result require their
   own geometry.
6. The active equipment configuration must resolve to applicable mechanical
   authority.
7. A correction cannot execute without an explicit Target Profile binding.
8. Backend remains the calculation authority; frontend code does not recreate
   geometry or click math.

These checks create or protect the result. They survive. A check whose only
purpose was to re-prove accepted printer authority does not.

## Expanded Learning

Every learned target contributes reusable assets:

- identity vocabulary and recognition landmarks;
- UGO mappings and orientation evidence;
- family geometry and procedure;
- equipment/mechanical branches;
- confirmation behavior.

The next conforming target supplies only facts that are actually new. It does
not repeat prior family proofs because its ink, logo, SKU, wording, or carrier
paper differs.
