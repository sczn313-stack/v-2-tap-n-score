> **One target. One identity. One coherent experience.**

## TSTT

**TSTT — Talking Smart Target Training** is the target-specific training layer. Each Smart Target receives one TSTT session divided into task-based chapters. A chapter is a complete learning unit defined by what the shooter must understand, rehearse, do, or change—not by arbitrary playback duration.

Phone-first controls are:

- `PAUSE`
- `REPLAY CHAPTER`
- `CHAPTERS`
- `RESTART TRAINING`

There is no 10-second rewind control.

The shooter may pause, replay a chapter, and physically rehearse a safe procedure or equipment adjustment before firing. The target-specific progression is:

> **Teach → Rehearse → Shoot → Measure → Adjust → Confirm → Preserve**

At the governed transition, TSTT hands into:

> **ADD PHOTO → Precision Tap → governed correction → adjustment → confirmation → SEC → Vault**

TSTT may explain and present resolved authority. TSTT never creates, repairs, generalizes, or substitutes authority.

## Authority ownership

- Equipment Authority owns reusable mechanical facts.
- Target Authority owns applicability, target-specific overrides, target procedure, correction calculation, and governed execution.
- Instructional/context copy presently printed on a conventional target may move into TSTT when that improves timing and comprehension.
- All authority-bearing information must remain traceable and must be delivered before the shooter needs to act on it.
- Missing or ambiguous authority remains visible as a narrow withheld action, not an invented lesson.

## Product identity chain

Each standalone Smart Target owns:

> **Publisher/Product Identity → Exact Face → Physical Geometry → Applicable Equipment/Mechanical Authority → QR Identity → TSTT → Governed Tap-n-Score Road**

Shared geometry does not merge products. Shared equipment facts do not grant target applicability. Shared TSTT machinery does not merge chapter language.

## Government and institutional evaluation thesis

Evaluation must measure **total cost to confirmed zero**, not target price alone. Future governed evaluation should be capable of measuring:

- rounds to confirmed zero;
- shot groups required;
- elapsed time;
- instructor intervention;
- confirmation outcome and evidence.

These metrics create evidence from which ammunition and training savings may be quantified. Savings must not be assumed or advertised before governed evidence supports them.
