## Rule 1 — Founder specification approval

> No implementation begins until the specification is Founder Approved.

The implementation contract must state what will change, what will not change, the affected surfaces, the validation plan, and the stopping point.

## Rule 2 — No Open Implementation Items

An implementation item is not closed until every lifecycle gate is complete. The next implementation item may not begin while another implementation item remains open.

## Founder Render Freeze Rule

After the Founder approves a render, only the approved observation may change.

- One observation in. One observation out.
- No bonus improvements.
- Every change earns its authorization.
- Local evidence proves implementation. Production evidence proves release.

## Presentation Governance — Eight Principles

### 1. Reality gets the final vote

Everything begins with evidence.

### 2. Truth comes from authority. Trust comes from explanation

Authority determines what is true. Presentation helps the shooter understand it.

### 3. The backend speaks in authority

The backend owns truth, eligibility, capability, scoring, Session Authority, and Evidence Authority. Its responsibility is correctness, traceability, and auditability.

### 4. The frontend speaks in guidance

The frontend answers:

1. Can I use this?
2. What can SCZN3 do for me?
3. What cannot it do?
4. What should I do next?

Its responsibility is clarity, confidence, and usability.

### 5. Evidence precedes explanation

Every explanation shall be visibly supported by authoritative evidence.

### 6. Presentation may evolve. Authority must not

Improving communication is encouraged. Changing truth requires governance.

### 7. Behavioral truth shall never be altered by presentation refinement

Presentation may change wording, labels, layout, explanations, and presentation assertions. Presentation shall not change authority, eligibility, capabilities, backend behavior, APIs, or behavioral validation.

### 8. A shooter should understand the outcome, not the architecture

The shooter should leave each screen understanding what happened, what SCZN3 determined, and what they can do next. They should not need to understand the architecture that produced the answer.
