# Episode 64 Founder Authority — SCZN3 X = 10 Ordered Grammar

Status: Founder Approved
Effective: 2026-10-03
Scope: Backend Authority and automatic Smart Target inheritance

## Governing rule

When an `X` center is the superior scoring region in an ordered scoring grammar such as:

`X / 9 / 8 / 7 / 6 / 5`

SCZN3 assigns:

`X = 10 points — SCZN3-derived Smart Target intelligence`

The interpretation is inherited by applicable canonical faces through the governed automatic-inheritance resolver. It is not printer truth unless independent publisher or governing evidence also establishes the same value.

## Eligibility

The rule applies only when all of these facts are governed:

1. `X` is a scoring region, not decorative text, an aim mark, a non-numeric tie-break count, or an unrelated symbol.
2. `X` is visibly the superior member of one ordered scoring grammar.
3. The numeric regions immediately beneath it form the descending sequence beginning at `9`.
4. The canonical-face treatment explicitly supplies that structural grammar to Backend Authority.

The letter `X`, a target name, OCR output, or visual resemblance alone is never sufficient inheritance authority.

## Precedence

Explicit publisher, governing-body, competition, or other controlling authority overrides SCZN3-derived treatment whenever it establishes a different numeric value or requires `X` to remain non-numeric.

The resolver records the winning authority and never silently overwrites it.

## Provenance

Every inherited result records:

- authority type: `sczn3_derived` or the controlling external authority;
- rule ID;
- the ordered grammar evaluated;
- derived or governed value;
- whether publisher attribution is permitted;
- the authority source and override decision.
