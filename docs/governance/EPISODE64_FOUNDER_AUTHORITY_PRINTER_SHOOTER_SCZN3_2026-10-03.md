# Episode 64 Founder Authority — Printer, Shooter, and SCZN3

Status: Founder Approved
Effective: 2026-10-03
Scope: Smart Target intelligence and Backend Authority provenance

## Governing principle

> **The printer defines and sells the physical paper target. The shooter owns the experience and may use that target as they see fit. SCZN3’s job is to make that shooting experience more useful, understandable, measurable, and enjoyable.**

Short form:

> **Printer defines the paper. Shooter chooses how to use it. SCZN3 makes the experience smarter.**

## Authority rule

SCZN3 is not limited to publisher-authored games or scoring procedures when adding Smart Target intelligence. It may create clearly identified **SCZN3-derived intelligence** from truthful visible target structure when all of the following remain true:

1. The derived rule is never attributed to the printer unless publisher evidence actually supports that attribution.
2. The derived rule does not overwrite contradictory publisher or governing authority.
3. The derived rule does not invent unsupported physical dimensions or hidden boundaries.
4. The derivation and its source evidence are transparent and stored with provenance as **SCZN3-derived**, not publisher truth.
5. Inheritance follows the same legitimate structural grammar and explicit governed treatment. Text matching, target names, or the mere presence of a symbol are not inheritance authority.

## Backend Authority separation

Every derived Smart Target treatment must preserve two distinct records:

- **Physical / publisher truth:** registered face, printed structure, published product facts, and publisher-authored rules actually supported by evidence.
- **SCZN3-derived intelligence:** the interpretation SCZN3 adds, its derivation, scope, provenance, contradictions checked, and the identities explicitly governed by that treatment.

Presentation may combine these for the shooter only when provenance remains unambiguous in Backend Authority.

## X treatment refinement

An undefined `X` remains non-numeric when no legitimate interpretation exists. That default must not prohibit a clearly labeled SCZN3-derived value when the visible structural grammar supports one and the derivation is explicitly governed.

There is no universal rule that `X = 10`. Any such value requires a target-treatment authority record. No executor, renderer, or catalog path may infer `10` from the character `X` alone.

## Founder #14 application

The Balloons canonical face visibly provides the ordered hierarchy:

`X / 9 / 8 / 7 / 6 / 5`

For that canonical face and its three governed identities, Founder authorizes:

`X = 10 points — SCZN3-derived scoring intelligence`

This is not a Baker-published rule. Baker remains authority for the physical Balloons face, product identity, eight balloon objects, printed X/9/8/7/6/5 structure, and its published balloon-popping objective. SCZN3 is authority only for the transparent derived interpretation of X as the next value in that visible descending hierarchy.

Outside observations remain count-only unless a separate explicit SCZN3 rule is later approved.

## Required safeguard

Targets containing an `X` do not inherit the Balloons derivation automatically. Inheritance requires the same legitimate structural grammar plus an explicit canonical-face treatment that cites this Founder authority. The shared executor consumes governed values; it does not create them.
