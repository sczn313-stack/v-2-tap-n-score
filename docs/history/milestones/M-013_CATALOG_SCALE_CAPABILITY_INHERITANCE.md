# M-013 — Catalog-Scale Capability Inheritance

**Status:** Founder Locked / Validated  
**Date:** 2026-08-19  
**Episode:** 56 — The Shortest Correct Mental Model

## Governing result

> **Target scale is not achieved by implementing more targets. It is achieved by identifying reusable shooter behaviors, assigning each behavior a governed owner, and allowing publisher products to inherit those capabilities through authoritative data.**

The governed composition is:

> **Publisher Product → authoritative identity / ATP → governed registration → Target Execution Contract → authorized capability/Profile → reusable backend owner → authoritative result → Universal SEC → preservation → exact reopen**

`targetProfileId + missionFamily` resolves one exact relationship. Products may expose multiple capabilities; execution never silently merges their authority.

## Evidence

- Batch 2: 12/12 previously unseen GunFun products inherited `smartEvidenceCapture`, Universal SEC, preservation, and exact reopen; target-ID production edits and target-specific production modules/routes were zero.
- GunFun full catalog: 237/258 distinct published SKUs completed the frozen generic lifecycle; 238 were association-ready including the preexisting Vital Dude path; exceptions remained explicit.
- GunFun + Baker capability analysis: 663 offerings; approximately 90% already mapped to demonstrated behavior; the remaining 69 collapsed into six missing behavioral families.
- Capability engine: Cue/Drill, Qualification, Diagnostic, Decision, SEC Progression, and Collection/Hand each passed a data-only third-fixture acceptance test with zero target-ID/SKU production wiring.
- Combined active implementation for the six owners: **59m 31s**.

## Locked boundaries

- Publisher Product Boundary and KTIS remain unchanged.
- Authority gaps are not architecture failures and may not be filled by inference.
- Shared primitives may be reused; authority may not be inherited or merged.
- A missing optional capability is not a missing target.
- Collection/Hand does not generalize into path, board, deck, casino, or generic-game behavior.
- Website entry is discovery-driven; QR entry is identity-driven; both use the same governed experience.
- Publisher OS publication, production QR generation, commit, push, deployment, and production promotion remain separately controlled and were not authorized by this milestone.

## Durable acceptance rule

> **A new authority-complete product must be capable of inheriting an existing applicable family through governed data without target-ID/SKU production-code edits.**

If that test fails, surface the shared-owner or architecture obstruction. Do not patch around it with another target branch.
