## Locked knowledge boundaries

> **Governed Target Specification ≠ Publisher Target Implementation Identity ≠ Commercial Target Offering.**

> **Organization Authority → Governed Target Specification → Publisher Implementation → Commercial SKU/package.**

An organization-governed specification may have several publisher implementations and many commercial offerings. Those layers do not create separate scoring engines. New authority-complete products inherit applicable SCZN3 capabilities through governed profiles and contract data, not target-ID/SKU production branches.

P-OS Publisher Intelligence applies the same precision-controlled Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 structure to all eleven publishers. Facts retain source, provenance, observation date, authority owner, evidence status, conflicts, and unknowns. Product labeling is not automatically endorsement, a retailer identifier is not a publisher SKU, and an observed commercial fact is not timeless.
