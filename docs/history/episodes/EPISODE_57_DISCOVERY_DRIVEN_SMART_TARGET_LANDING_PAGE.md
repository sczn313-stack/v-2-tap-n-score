## Problem

SCZN3 had accumulated governed target identities and reusable capability families, but the shooter-facing front door still exposed platform mechanics instead of making target discovery, photo entry, guidance, history, and publisher continuity immediately understandable.

## Discovery

The landing page is a discovery-driven front door with two entrances into the same governed platform:

> **CHOOSE OUR TARGET or SHOW US YOUR TARGET → LET THE TARGET TALK.**

The selected or recognized target determines what SCZN3 may truthfully do. Talk guides that relationship but creates no authority. Discovery can be simple while the platform underneath remains exact.

## Founder-approved result

- Two tappable entry containers: **CHOOSE OUR TARGET** and **SHOW US YOUR TARGET**; mobile presents photo entry first.
- Photo entry presents **Take Photo / Choose Existing Photo** before invoking a device interface.
- Shared promise: **LET THE TARGET TALK.** SCZN3 gave a voice to the target.
- Discovery instruction: **What do you want to shoot today? / Pick an activity, choose your target, then listen.**
- Eight activity doors: Diagnose & Zero, Practice, Compete, Qualify, Law Enforcement, Fun & Games, Just Shoot, and Military.
- An open door becomes **CLOSE ↑**; close collapses its choices and immediately stops its narration.
- A target identity appears once across the complete rendered discovery layout. Representative and additional targets remain distinct.
- Expanded choices use dedicated `shooterDescription`; backend/governance language does not leak into shooter copy or narration.
- Applicable entry inherits Standard equipment when needed.
- Ballistic Vault and contextual printer discovery are the two closing destinations; printer discovery follows exact publisher identity and fails closed when unavailable.
- Publisher products inherit capabilities through governed registration rather than target-specific application wiring.
