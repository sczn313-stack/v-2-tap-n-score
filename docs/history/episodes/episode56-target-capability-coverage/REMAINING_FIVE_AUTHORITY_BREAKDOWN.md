## Family findings

### CF-11 — Governed diagnostic feedback

**Reusable shape:** `Diagnostic Feedback Profile → Diagnostic Feedback Evaluator → governed result`

- **Publisher establishes:** Exact product identity and artwork; Baker expressly claims handedness-specific tutor feedback and example shooting causes, while GunFun identifies diagnostic zones/tags. Printed labels may be publisher evidence, but catalog metadata alone does not establish a complete structured mapping.
- **SCZN3 already recognizes:** Exact product identity, evidence capture, shot-observation reconciliation, SEC, preservation/reopen; registered geometry only where separately authorized.
- **Evidence-only today:** Record and preserve visible observations without diagnosing cause, technique, anatomy, or correction.
- **Smallest missing behavior:** Classify authorized observations into exact publisher-defined diagnostic regions and return only the corresponding publisher-supplied label, with provenance and confidence/failure state.
- **Authority gap:** Most GunFun catalog records establish a diagnostic tag or zone but do not publish the complete label/geometry mapping in structured authority. Baker describes example causes, but exact region mappings still require authoritative packaging from the target/Publisher Profile.
- **Subfamily result:** No. Right/left-handed layouts, humorous labels, bullseye versus silhouette presentation, and the Navy composition are profile variants. Navy qualification remains independently composed CF-12 authority.
- **Claim grades:** evidence_only → publisher_label_match → publisher_governed_feedback

### CF-13 — Commanded, cued, randomized, or timed drill orchestration

**Reusable shape:** `Cue/Drill Profile → Cue/Drill Orchestrator → governed result`

- **Publisher establishes:** Exact product identity and selectable printed entities such as shapes, colors, numbers, rounds, or aim points, plus intended command/timed/memory use. Most product pages do not establish one canonical cue sequence, timing model, completion rule, or result.
- **SCZN3 already recognizes:** Registered product, evidence capture, session state, backend-authoritative result, Universal SEC, preservation/reopen, and reusable scoring/challenge components where separately authorized.
- **Evidence-only today:** Record the target and observations; an instructor may conduct an external drill, but SCZN3 cannot claim it issued, timed, randomized, or evaluated that drill.
- **Smallest missing behavior:** Issue or accept governed cues, maintain sequence/timer/memory state, associate observations with the active cue, reconcile correct/late/missed/extra responses, and terminate by profile rules.
- **Authority gap:** Many Command products publish useful selectable shapes/colors/numbers but no canonical course. Primary links sample Simon-Says-style instructions; Fusion links a mutable external drill library. Each executable drill still needs a frozen, versioned rules profile.
- **Subfamily result:** One family with declarative modes: commanded selection, randomized cue, fixed timed string, and memory/repetition. CTQ composes CF-12 qualification; it does not create a new orchestrator.
- **Claim grades:** evidence_only → externally_conducted_drill_evidence → sczn3_orchestrated_governed_drill

### CF-14 — Decision discrimination and scenario state

**Reusable shape:** `Decision Scenario Profile → Decision Scenario Evaluator → governed result`

- **Publisher establishes:** Exact Good/Bad paired identity and the publisher's shoot/no-shoot designation for Dan, Dave, Jeff, and Rich products.
- **SCZN3 already recognizes:** Exact target identity, evidence capture, observation reconciliation, session/SEC lifecycle, and preservation.
- **Evidence-only today:** Record observations against the exact product without inferring threat, legality, tactics, anatomy, or whether firing was correct.
- **Smallest missing behavior:** Govern which scenario identity was presented, the authorized expected response including no-response, response window, observation association, transition, and truthful result semantics.
- **Authority gap:** Baker establishes the pair membership and Good/Bad shoot/don't-shoot concept, but the catalog does not establish a complete digital course: presentation order, timing, shot limits, penalties, completion, or scoring.
- **Subfamily result:** No. Four character pairs are data instances of the same scenario-state behavior. Any future legal, tactical, use-of-force, or multi-scene judgment would require separate external authority and may expand the family later.
- **Claim grades:** evidence_only → publisher_designation_recall → governed_scenario_response_result

### CF-15 — Longitudinal progression

**Reusable shape:** `Progression Profile → SEC Progression Evaluator → governed result`

- **Publisher establishes:** Baker establishes that the Bullseye Challenge target is used with its 20-page guide to track accuracy and advance from lower to higher levels over time.
- **SCZN3 already recognizes:** Individual session evidence/results, shooter/session identity where governed, Universal SEC, preservation, and exact historical reopen.
- **Evidence-only today:** Preserve each target session independently; the shooter may consult the physical guide, but SCZN3 cannot assign or advance a level.
- **Smallest missing behavior:** Evaluate eligible preserved SECs against versioned level criteria, maintain current level and history, advance or hold by exact rules, and preserve the profile/version that produced each transition.
- **Authority gap:** The public product page establishes progression intent but not the guide's complete level criteria. The 20-page Baker guide is the primary rules authority and must be obtained, versioned, and licensed/approved for digital use.
- **Subfamily result:** No subfamily is established by one lineage. Architecturally this is a cross-session evaluator over SECs, not a target executor. A second independent progression fixture is required before claiming broad generality.
- **Claim grades:** independent_session_history → publisher_program_progress → credential_or_rank_only_if_separately_authorized

### CF-16 — Declarative collection/hand/path game state

**Reusable shape:** `Collection/Hand Game Profile → Collection/Hand Evaluator → governed result`

- **Publisher establishes:** Baker establishes Poker Wheel play, solo or multiplayer use, poker-hand formation, Royal Flush objective, and an instruction guide printed on the target.
- **SCZN3 already recognizes:** Target identity, evidence capture, observation reconciliation, multiplayer/session primitives from existing target-shaped games, Universal SEC, and preservation.
- **Evidence-only today:** Record the impacts and preserve the evidence; the shooter may determine the poker hand manually from the printed guide.
- **Smallest missing behavior:** Map observations to card/collection members, enforce hand size and turn rules, evaluate combinations/rank/ties, maintain player state, and terminate according to the exact printed game rules.
- **Authority gap:** The product description is insufficient for executable details such as observation-to-card mapping, duplicate treatment, shot/hand size, turn structure, ties, misses, and completion. The printed guide must be captured as authoritative profile data.
- **Subfamily result:** Only collection/hand evaluation is evidenced by the counted products. The broader 'path' portion is not yet proven by this 69-offering denominator and must not be prebuilt. One lineage is insufficient to prove a universal game executor.
- **Claim grades:** evidence_only → publisher_hand_result → governed_multiplayer_game_result

## Cross-family architecture conclusion

The six missing families still stand after product-level inspection, but their correct shapes are now clearer:

1. Diagnostic Feedback Profile → Diagnostic Feedback Evaluator.
2. Qualification Profile → Qualification Executor, with official administration/attestation separate.
3. Cue/Drill Profile → Cue/Drill Orchestrator.
4. Decision Scenario Profile → Decision Scenario Evaluator.
5. Progression Profile → SEC Progression Evaluator.
6. Collection/Hand Game Profile → Collection/Hand Evaluator.

CF-15 and CF-16 have only one product lineage each. Their boundaries are plausible but not yet empirically reusable; each needs a second independent authority-complete fixture before generality is accepted. No path-game owner is justified yet.
