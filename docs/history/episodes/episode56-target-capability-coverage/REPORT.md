**Status:** Investigation complete; Founder review

**Scope:** Capability analysis only

## Executive answer

> **SCZN3 has demonstrated 10 of 16 distinct capability families established by the current GunFun and Baker catalog evidence. Four of the 16 are already reusable capability owners suitable for registration-driven inheritance; six demonstrated families still require extraction from target-shaped implementations; and six families are genuinely absent. Closing those 12 reusable-owner gaps once would provide a capability-composition path for 100% of the 663 catalog-evidenced individual target-product offerings, or 100% of the 479 product lineages after Baker bulk-packaging duplicates are collapsed, without additional target-ID production implementation.**

This is architectural coverage, not execution authority. A product still fails closed until its exact Target Profile, geometry, rules, mission, and other required authority are available. No missing authority was inferred to improve the result.

## 2. What counts as a capability family

A capability family is a reusable governed behavior whose result, authority inputs, state transitions, SEC meaning, or preservation requirements differ materially from another family.

Artwork, color, paper size, quantity, bulk packaging, silhouette subject, splatter ink, publisher category, and a different number of otherwise equivalent aim points do not create a new capability family.

A family is counted as represented only when publisher or existing SCZN3 authority establishes the behavior. Product names or visual suggestions alone do not authorize scoring, anatomy, tactical meaning, qualification, timing, penalties, or mission rules.

Each family is classified by maturity:

- **Reusable owner:** an existing shared owner can accept registration/contract data without target-ID production routing.
- **Demonstrated but target-shaped:** SCZN3 has delivered the behavior, but the implementation is still bound to a named target or bespoke authority module and must be extracted before an analogous target can inherit it.
- **Genuinely missing:** no current SCZN3 capability owner truthfully delivers the behavior.

## 6. Authority watchlist — not counted as new families

The catalogs contain labels or artwork that may later establish additional requirements, but present evidence is insufficient to count them as capability families:

- **Shotgun pattern analysis:** a shotgun target exists, but the publisher catalog does not establish pellet-density, coverage, choke, distance, or pass/fail rules.
- **USPSA/IDPA hit factor or official match execution:** products identify target forms, but a target product page does not itself authorize stages, round counts, timing, penalties, hit factor, classification, or official claims.
- **Anatomical, medical, tactical, or hunting significance:** printed organs, bones, hostage scenes, or “critical” labels do not authorize SCZN3 interpretation.
- **Multi-physical-target arrays:** packs and bundles identify commerce relationships, not one executable target mission or synchronized multi-target course.
- **Automatic reactive/splatter interpretation:** reactive ink improves visible evidence but supplies no distinct digital scoring authority.

If authoritative publisher or governing-body rules later establish one of these as behavior not expressible by CF-01 through CF-16, reality increases the denominator. The current result must not pre-authorize it.

## 7. Recommended architecture sequence

Do not reopen Publisher OS implementation yet.

The smallest leverage-oriented sequence is:

1. Extract a declarative Course/Drill substrate from CF-06 and CF-07.
2. Add CF-13 cue/timed orchestration on that substrate.
3. Add CF-12 qualification as a versioned evaluator over Course/Drill plus scoring authority.
4. Extract a declarative Game State substrate from CF-08 through CF-10.
5. Add CF-16 collection/hand/path rules to Game State.
6. Add CF-14 decision scenarios using cue/session orchestration and exact scenario authority.
7. Add CF-11 diagnostic feedback as a narrowly governed analysis owner.
8. Add CF-15 progression over Universal SEC and preserved-session identity.

This sequence implements shared substrates once while keeping each capability owner and authority boundary explicit.

## 8. Acceptance test for capability completeness

For each family, a synthetic or authority-complete fixture must prove:

- the product becomes playable through registration and contract data with zero target-ID production edits;
- the backend result is authoritative and family-specific;
- missing, incompatible, suspended, or incomplete authority fails closed;
- the Universal SEC truthfully presents only the family’s authorized results;
- preservation and exact reopen retain target, publisher, family, authority, and result identity;
- two different products inherit the same family owner without identity leakage;
- adding a third fixture requires data only;
- no optional missing capability makes the registered target disappear.

The final cross-catalog acceptance test is a frozen mixed cohort containing at least one previously unseen product for every family represented by available authority.

## Governed conclusion

The ten-target baseline did cover most of the behavioral gamut: **10 of 16 families are proven**. But only **four of 16 are presently reusable at the zero-target-code scale boundary**.

The next architecture milestone is therefore not another catalog batch. It is:

> Six demonstrated behaviors extracted into reusable owners + six genuinely new reusable capability owners → all currently evidenced GunFun and Baker target products composable by authority and data.

Publisher OS remains on the starting line until this capability engine is Founder specified, approved, implemented, and validated.

No application code, Publisher OS mechanism, target registration, QR asset, production change, commit, push, or deployment was created by this analysis.
