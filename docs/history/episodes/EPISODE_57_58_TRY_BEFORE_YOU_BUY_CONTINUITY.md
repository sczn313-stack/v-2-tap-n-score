**Status:** Discovery preserved for a future Founder Specification; not implemented

## Shooter relationship

> **Don't understand it? Listen to it. Not sure you'll like it? Try it. Like it? Buy it.**

The intended approximately 1.5–2 minute bridge is:

> **Discover/choose → Try → Equipment Library → active equipment → SCZN3 experience → SEC → Ballistic Vault → returning reuse → Rate Us**

The Equipment Library is not a detour in this flow. It prevents the shooter from entering the same firearm/configuration facts again during the trial and on later sessions.

> **Add it once. Use it whenever you shoot.**

## Continuity principles

- **Listen** may be unlimited and shareable because guidance does not itself grant executable authority.
- **Try** is envisioned as one hands-on practice session per shooter/target, subject to a future governed identity/entitlement specification.
- **Buy** returns the shooter to the physical target/publisher relationship.
- The selected active Equipment Profile may supply applicable known equipment facts without bypassing Session Authority.
- The completed experience produces an authoritative result only within the target/mission capability actually available.
- The Universal SEC preserves the session; Ballistic Vault makes the history reusable and reopenable.
- Equipment Library, SEC, and Ballistic Vault are three views of one continuing shooter relationship, not isolated features.
- A returning shooter reuses the saved active equipment rather than repeating setup.

## Rate Us continuity

Rate Us should be available after completion and later through universal navigation. It remains voluntary, optional, and first-party:

> **How was your SCZN3 experience?**  
> **1–5 stars**

A low rating may expose one optional short feedback opportunity. No survey is authorized, and no new feedback backend is required.
