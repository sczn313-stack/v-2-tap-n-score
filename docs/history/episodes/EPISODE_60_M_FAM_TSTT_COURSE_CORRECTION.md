# Episode 60 — M/FAM / TSTT Course Correction

Date: 2026-09-02

Status: Founder Approved / architecture and governance preserved / implementation pending

## Discovery

The M/FAM program is not a two-sided-paper implementation problem. It is a three-product learning system. Each exact target needs one standalone Smart identity and one coherent training-to-evidence road, while Baker's conventional products remain intact beside the premium Smart versions.

Founder-supplied Baker evidence also resolved the Universal MOA branch: `ST-Uni25mMOA` uses the protected Fort Benning/Founder physical geometry and printed sight table but remains an independently branded Baker product. Its classification is `SAME_GEOMETRY_NEW_PRODUCT`.

## Founder decisions

- Initial lineup: Baker Universal MOA 25 Meter, Baker M4 25 Meter, and Baker M16A2 25 Meter.
- Fort Benning is preserved evidence/authority/road lineage, not a separate commercial product project.
- Each Smart product is standalone and single-sided.
- Conventional Baker targets remain separate products and may be merchandised side-by-side with Smart versions.
- TSTT — Talking Smart Target Training — is the target-specific training layer.
- TSTT chapters are complete task-based learning units.
- Phone-first navigation is `PAUSE`, `REPLAY CHAPTER`, `CHAPTERS`, and `RESTART TRAINING`; there is no 10-second rewind.
- Core progression is **Teach → Rehearse → Shoot → Measure → Adjust → Confirm → Preserve**.
- TSTT hands into Tap-n-Score at `ADD PHOTO` and may explain, but never invent, authority.
- Equipment Authority/Target Authority ownership from Snapshot 20 remains unchanged.
- Year-One business architecture begins from base-target economics plus an incremental Smart premium allocated 60% SCZN3 / 40% printer, subject to agreement and without mandatory resale pricing.
- Government/institutional evaluation should measure total cost to confirmed zero using rounds, groups, elapsed time, instructor intervention, and confirmation evidence.
