# Episode 60 — TSTT Founder Review Pass #4

**Date:** 2026-09-03

**Status:** Founder-review candidate implemented and verified

Founder cold review exposed that equipment confirmation had become a front-of-class permission gate even though the lesson could truthfully identify its own governed setup. Pass #4 removes that gate and makes training immediately discoverable in the fixed PLAYER rail.

The shooter now sees the complete M16A2 target, concise M16A2 standard-iron training guidance, and an always-visible `START TRAINING` action. Equipment remains visible and editable through Equipment Library, but no confirmation is required to learn. Different equipment may enter the course; M16A2-specific values remain explicitly bounded to the named training setup and are not granted as authority for other gear.

The reusable platform result is recorded in `TSTT_TARGET_SPECIFIC_TRAINING_ARCHITECTURE.md`: lesson access is permissive, while equipment-specific execution authority remains strict.

The rear-sight visual/rehearsal discovery, missing general-zeroing authority, live-fire road, activation, push, and deployment remain outside this pass.
