## Reconciled outcome

Episode 56 converted the catalog-scale question into a governed operating model:

> **Publisher products inherit reusable SCZN3 capabilities through authoritative data; they do not become individual software implementations.**

The complete relationship is:

> **Publisher Product → authoritative identity / ATP → governed registration → Target Execution Contract → authorized capability/Profile → reusable backend owner → authoritative result → Universal SEC → preservation → exact reopen**

### Scale architecture

- Batch 1 established the pre-architecture baseline and preserved work by cause.
- Vital Dude proved the Publisher Product Boundary through an evidence-only playable flow without invented target meaning.
- The Target Execution Scale Architecture proved two distinct products could inherit one family and SEC path with zero target-ID production routing.
- A blind Batch 2 processed 12/12 products through backend result, Universal SEC, preservation, and exact reopen with zero target-ID edits or target-specific production modules/routes.
- The complete 258-SKU GunFun stress test admitted 237 products through the frozen architecture. Vital Dude remained valid on its preexisting governed route; 3 products remained publisher-authority holds and 17 commercial offerings were correctly excluded as non-single-target representations.
- Publisher OS / full-catalog QR feasibility was specified but not implemented. It remains behind a separate Founder implementation gate.

### Capability completeness

GunFun + Baker analysis examined 663 offerings. Approximately 90% mapped to demonstrated behavior. The remaining 69 offerings collapsed into six missing behavioral families rather than 69 implementations.

Reusable governed owners were implemented and validated for:

1. Cue/Drill Orchestration;
2. Qualification Course Evaluation;
3. Diagnostic Feedback Evaluation;
4. Decision Scenario Evaluation;
5. SEC Progression Evaluation;
6. Collection/Hand Evaluation.

All six passed data-only inheritance tests with zero target-ID/SKU production wiring. Combined active implementation time: **59m 31s**.

### Durable boundaries

- registration establishes the digital relationship; authority determines capabilities;
- multi-capability does not mean multi-authority execution;
- shared primitives may be reused, but authority cannot be silently inherited;
- authority gaps are not architecture failures;
- website entry is discovery-driven and QR entry is identity-driven, converging on one governed experience;
- missing scoring, qualification, diagnostic, government, licensed-guide, official-administration, subject-continuity, or other authority fails closed;
- Publisher OS implementation, production QR issuance, production deployment, commit, and push remain outside this closeout.
