## The problem

The current selector is shallow and mixes category, form factor, family, model, caliber, and commercial identity. The desired system can know the exact firearm, sight/optic, mount/configuration, ammunition, adjustment behavior, zero/target relationship, and history while keeping the shooter interaction small.

> **Make the knowledge underneath enormous. Make the shooter's interaction with it tiny.**

M4/M4A1 proved that adjustment behavior belongs to the selected configuration—not a generic `1/4 MOA` platform default—and that 25-meter zeroing and distance confirmation are distinct evidence.
