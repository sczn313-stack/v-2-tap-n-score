# QT Homie Smart Catalog — Artwork-Augmented Review

**Rule:** stored authority first; the target's own artwork is additional authority for what is visibly printed — regions, objects, labels, numbers, X-rings, aim points, diagnostic sectors, grids, and other explicit structure. Artwork does **not** create unpublished timing, sequence, qualification thresholds, penalties, or course-of-fire rules.

Population: **254 / 254** canonical QT surfaces.

## Catalog-wide intelligence families

- **VALUED_REGION_QUALIFICATION — 71 targets:** Capture every visibly bounded/labeled region or object. If the face itself prints numeric point values, score them; otherwise count by region/object. Report totals, distribution, misses, pattern, and history. Do not invent a qualification threshold.
- **EVIDENCE_ONLY — 63 targets:** Preserve every tap at the exact source-image location. Report total taps, shot-pattern center, dispersion/distribution and history. Upgrade only when the face itself provides bindable regions/objects/values.
- **B_FAMILY_SCORING — 34 targets:** Capture every tap against the printed B-family regions. Score only the numeric/X values visibly printed or otherwise stored. Report total taps, region counts, points, X-count when present, misses, distribution, shot-pattern center, and session history.
- **HOSTAGE_THREAT_RELATIONSHIPS — 29 targets:** Capture visibly distinct figures/regions/objects exactly as printed. Count hits by printed/visible object or governed region and show misses and distribution. Do not invent tactical success, threat priority, penalties, or engagement order.
- **DISCRIMINATION_OBJECTS — 13 targets:** Capture each printed number/color/shape/object as its own object. Report hits by object, untouched objects, total taps, misses, distribution, and history. Do not invent a sequence unless printed/stored.
- **BULLSEYE_MULTI_BULL — 11 targets:** Capture each bull/spot independently, including visible center/aim-point subsets. Count hits by bull and center subset; score rings only when values are printed/stored. Report balance across bulls, misses, pattern, and history.
- **REPEATED_OBJECT_COUNTING — 10 targets:** Capture each repeated dot/object independently. Report per-object hits, objects used/unused, total taps, misses, distribution/evenness, and repeat-session progress. No invented shot quota or sequence.
- **M16_M4_QUALIFICATION — 9 targets:** Capture every printed aim area/qualification object. Count or score only what the face/stored authority proves. M16A2 keeps the governed zeroing road. Do not invent qualification thresholds or stage rules.
- **ZEROING_SIGHT_IN — 7 targets:** Capture printed aiming/scoring structures. Use governed zeroing correction only where the stored zeroing authority exists; otherwise provide counts/score from printed values plus shot-pattern center and displacement observations.
- **COMPETITION_SCORING — 3 targets:** Capture printed competition zones/objects. Score only printed/stored values; otherwise zone count. Report totals, zone distribution, misses, pattern, and history. No invented match procedure.
- **ANATOMY_VITAL_REGIONS — 2 targets:** Capture visibly printed/labeled anatomical regions only where boundaries can be bound to the artwork. Report region counts and pattern; no invented medical, tactical, or lethality meaning.
- **DIAGNOSTIC_FEEDBACK — 2 targets:** Capture printed scoring rings plus printed diagnostic sectors. Score the visible numeric rings and report the sector(s) containing impacts as artwork-derived feedback. No invented causal diagnosis beyond the target's printed wording.
