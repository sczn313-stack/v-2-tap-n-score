## Delta finding

The closer-look queue collapsed to data bindings over existing primitives. OPOTA RQT-2 has the strongest external governing authority located: official Ohio sources publish region values, stage constraints, rounds, timing, and pass thresholds, but those sources are historical and require current-version confirmation before admission. QT’s Shootin Aces article publishes “find the hidden aces,” but no score/course. The remaining partial families publish artwork structure without a complete course.

DIAG-L/R and SI-2/3/4 are resolved inheritance: handedness mirrors diagnostic labels; bull count changes sight-in geometry. Neither creates a new experience.
