# M/FAM Authority Simplification 10

Date: 2026-09-08

## Directive

> **AUTHORITY = AUTHORITY.**
>
> **KEEP THE KNOWLEDGE. DELETE THE BUREAUCRACY. SCALE.**

This pass removed runtime machinery whose only function was to re-authorize
printer, family, registration, and mechanical authority already represented by
an explicit governed Target Profile binding.

## Runtime chain after simplification

```text
Printer Authority
  -> Recognition
    -> UGO registration/orientation
      -> Equipment Authority
        -> Backend calculation
          -> Tap-n-Score result and universal zeroing road
```

Family records remain reusable geometry, aim, procedure, mechanics, and
confirmation knowledge. They are data compression, not another approval stage.

## Expanded Learning rule

New target knowledge is additive:

- recognition landmarks improve later recognition;
- UGO mappings improve later representation mapping;
- family facts are reused, not re-proved;

equipment applicability, or the governed result.
