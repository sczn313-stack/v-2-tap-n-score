## Registration and authority boundary

The batch did not reuse one printer's artifact as another printer's registration. Each admitted face has its own exact canonical asset, hash, registration package, asymmetric orientation anchors, UGO authority, and Target Profile binding. The shared Backend family resolver now accepts any proven family-permitted mechanical profile. No M4 mechanics were copied into Baker-specific code, and no M16A2 mechanics were copied into GunFun-specific code.
