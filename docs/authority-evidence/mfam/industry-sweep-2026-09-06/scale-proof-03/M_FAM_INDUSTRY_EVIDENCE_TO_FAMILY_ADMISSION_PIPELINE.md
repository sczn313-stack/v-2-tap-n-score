# Industry Evidence → Family Admission Pipeline

This pattern converts public industry evidence into governed Target Profile admission without rebuilding a target experience.

## 1. Resolve the exact identity

Record the publisher or issuing authority, product/SKU/NSN, physical side, and representation separately. A two-sided product never becomes one face.

Output: `Publisher → Product → Face → Representation`.

## 2. Reuse the family facts already governed

Evaluate the face against the family contract. Reuse family geometry, aim, procedure, mechanical branch, and confirmation only when preserved evidence traceably proves conformance. Do not re-prove those facts per publisher.

Output: a six-gate Family Conformance result with every inherited evidence package named.

## 3. Acquire only the irreducible evidence gap

Search in this order:

1. preserved SCZN3 publisher/government corpus;
2. publisher or manufacturer primary page/file;
3. official government production artifact or doctrine figure;
4. authoritative calibrated drawing or production PDF.

A thumbnail may prove representation identity. It may not prove physical pitch. A flat digital plate may prove exact representation and orientation. It may not prove physical scale unless its provenance or dimensions establish that claim.

Output: hashed evidence artifact with source quality and a narrow `proves` list.

## 4. Establish the highest truthful reusable bridge

If an NSN, issued-form identity, or other primary relationship governs several products, preserve one reusable evidence package. The package must state its limits so identity and reverse-side authority do not spread accidentally.

Output: reusable evidence package, such as `NSN-6920-01-395-2949-PRODUCTION-REPRESENTATION-BRIDGE-v1`.

## 5. Build exact-representation registration

Every publisher/issuer representation receives its own:

- canonical asset and SHA-256;
- target profile and version;
- canonical coordinate system;
- grid/aim mapping;
- asymmetric TOP/BOTTOM/LEFT/RIGHT anchor set;
- coverage, match, inlier, and reprojection gates;
- source-evidence directional reconciliation.

Exact representation registration is never inherited from another publisher.

Output: one Target Image Registration package per exact representation.

## 6. Admit through data, not workflow

Create one explicit Target Profile binding:

`Exact Product/Face → Registration Package → Family Authority Package → Universal Zeroing Capability`

The product contributes identity and representation. The family contributes geometry, aim, procedure, mechanics, and confirmation. The platform contributes photo entry, Precision Tap, results, correction presentation, confirmation, SEC, Vault, and recovery states.

Output: executable binding with no target-specific zeroing implementation.

## 7. Preserve identity through the result

The backend session envelope and SEC adapter must carry publisher, product, identifiers, face, registration, family authority, and mechanical authority. Ballistic Vault reopening must recover that exact identity rather than a generic family label.

## 8. Fail closed at the fact—not at the product

If a gate remains unresolved, retain the known target identity and report one exact missing artifact or fact. Do not say “take another photo” when the failure is missing geometry, procedure, mechanical applicability, or production evidence.

## Admission economics

Track these values on every family pass:

- authoritative artifacts acquired;
- reusable evidence packages created;
- representation packages created;
- new executable bindings;
- family leverage;
- admission leverage;
- product-specific implementations (must remain zero for inherited zeroing targets);
- new universal roads (must remain zero).

The scale test passes when the marginal work is evidence + registration + binding, not another application.
