# M/FAM: Exact Commonality vs Genuine Difference

Date: 2026-09-06

## Proven common

| Pair | What is common | What remains separate |
|---|---|---|
| Protected modern Universal ↔ Baker `ST-Uni25mMOA` | 32 × 32 quarter-inch lattice, 8 × 8 grid, center, rings and spatial geometry | publisher product, exact representation, registration, selected sight/configuration |
| Protected modern Universal ↔ National `23112` no-yellow face | lattice, rings, center references and table layout shown by publisher face evidence | publisher product, production-scale registration, configuration authority |
| National `23112` no-yellow ↔ yellow-circle | physical geometry and landmarks | face representation/ink and registration variant |
| Standard M16A2 face ↔ exact Baker standard representation | 18 × 20, 0.96 cm Army geometry and standard procedure | Baker product identity remains explicit |
| All admitted zero targets | universal SCZN3 interaction and preservation road | every target/profile difference listed below |

## Proven different

| Pair | Genuine difference | Required consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Standard M16A2 ↔ standard M4 | 18 × 20 / 0.96 cm versus 14 × 16 / 1.3 cm; sight mechanics differ | separate geometry, registration, procedure/config binding |
| Standard M16A2 ↔ modern Universal | grid count/pitch, landmarks, aim/offset treatment, tables | separate Target Profiles |
| Standard M16A2 ↔ Baker `ST-M16A2R` | 8.5 × 11 versus manufactured 23 × 35 scale | new physical geometry/profile; no registration inheritance |
| 25m live-fire zero ↔ 10m boresight offset | distance, aim/strike relationship and device procedure | separate target family |
| 25m Army zero ↔ USMC 36m BZO | procedure and distance | separate family; exact USMC face required |
| standard zero ↔ Alt-C/Navy qualification/slow/timed fire | mission, scoring/measurement regions and procedure | separate qualification/composite families |
| True-MOA ↔ True-MIL | angular coordinate unit/geometry | separate target profiles |
| M16A2 iron ↔ M4 iron ↔ optic/laser rows | physical controls and click authority | separate mechanical authority records even if a face is shared |

## Unproven—not “probably common”

- Rite in the Rain `9125` faces versus Baker/Army standard faces.
- Qualification Targets M16A2/M4 versus other standard products.
- Speedwell `DODM4` versus Baker or issued production sheets.
- Alco `AR-0` versus standard M16A2 at physical print scale.
- GunFun red versus green and either versus a standard face.
- Action `MOA-25-1S` and Rite `9126` versus the protected Universal geometry.
- Army A8 production face versus the protected SCZN3 modern representation.
- Embedded zero subfaces inside Navy/composite sheets.

Similar ink does not close these claims. Exact product/side evidence, physical measurement, and orientation-capable registration are required.

## Scaling answer

What is truly common is the governed zeroing capability and, for proven products, a physical geometry family. What genuinely differs is supplied as data:

- product and face identity;
- representation;
- page scale, grid, aim and landmarks;
- zero/final distance and procedure;
- weapon/sight/optic/laser configuration;
- per-control mechanical authority;
- registration/orientation package;
- target-specific TSTT language.

That boundary allows a new printer’s proven copy of an existing geometry to reuse measurement and workflow without pretending it is the same product or automatically granting correction authority.
