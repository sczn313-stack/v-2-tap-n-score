# Baker-Comparable Target Market Study

Observation date: **2026-09-01**

Scope: the complete preserved P-OS printer set — the 11-printer master plus additive Publisher #12, Thompson Target Inc.

This is a current retail observation and fee-sensitivity study, not a pricing decision. Shipping, tax, distributor margin, and sales weighting are excluded. Official publisher/store pages are the source of price and package quantity. Where only multipacks are sold, the study shows both the actual pack price and a transparent per-physical-target normalization.

## Formula

- `current per target = official current pack price / physical target sheets in pack`
- `proposed retail = current per target + $0.50`
- `% increase = $0.50 / current per target`
- `SCZN3 share % = $0.25 / current per target`
- `printer share % = $0.25 / current per target`

The proposed $0.50 is modeled as $0.25 for SCZN3 plus $0.25 for the printer. It is not represented as an approved commercial policy.

## What the numbers say

- The same flat $0.50 has radically different commercial weight across the market. It adds 10% to Birchwood Casey's selected premium reactive product, 27.47% to Baker's Smart Target, and more than 100% to seven selected low-cost/bulk-normalized products.
- Baker's current $1.82 single Smart Target is already above most selected normalized paper-target prices, but below the selected Birchwood Casey long-range reactive target.
- Institutional bulk products make a flat per-sheet charge look disproportionately large. National's temporary sale produces the most extreme result; its regular $17.50/100 list price would normalize to $0.175 and a 285.71% increase, still material.
- These are product/channel observations, not publisher-wide averages. Reactive coatings, dimensions, stock, pack size, and channel differ; the table intentionally avoids pretending they are identical products.
