# Revision Notes

## Revision 2 — Founder-approved knowledge philosophy

Revision 2 changes the epistemic model of the first synthesis.

Previous framing treated shooter improvement primarily as a governed loop that
applied exact source-owned meaning. That language correctly protected product
execution boundaries, but it incorrectly made Publisher OS authority packaging
the philosophy of general SCZN3 Shooting Knowledge.

Revision 2 establishes:

> **Shooter improvement is a learning loop: define the shooter's objective,
> observe repeatable evidence, apply the best relevant knowledge SCZN3 has
> synthesized, make one useful change, compare the result, and learn from what
> happens next.**

And:

> **MINE → COMMONIZE → RECONCILE → SYNTHESIZE → DELIVER → LEARN**

The twelve publishers are now explicitly treated as knowledge inputs. There is
no governing-authority hierarchy for general shooting knowledge. The output is organized around
shooters, concepts, observable problems, improvement methods, measurement,
progression, and useful next actions.

Source provenance remains preserved underneath for evidence integrity,
validation, licensing, historical correction, and specialized official-rule
claims. It is not the product and does not organize the knowledge base.

No underlying corpus evidence was deleted, no product capability was activated,
and no production behavior changed.
