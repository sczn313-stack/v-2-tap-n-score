claim that every commercial record contains instructional knowledge.

The sources below are evidence inputs, not the organization of the knowledge
product and not a hierarchy for general shooting knowledge. SCZN3 commonizes
what they collectively support, expresses the synthesis originally, and keeps
provenance underneath for validation, integrity, licensing, and correction.

## Precision rules carried into the synthesis

1. Product labeling is not organizational endorsement.
2. Possible experience is not an authorized capability.
3. Compatibility is not instruction.
4. Reactive visibility is not diagnosis or proof of improvement.
5. A reduced target face is not every condition of the full-distance event.
6. A target is not a qualification course or official credential.
7. Publisher-authored guidance remains attributed to the publisher.
8. A catalog observation is dated evidence, not a timeless fact.
9. Repeated SKUs, quantities, materials and printer implementations do not
   become repeated knowledge claims.
10. Public visibility does not establish a license to reproduce, speak, cache,
    or operationalize protected instructional content.
