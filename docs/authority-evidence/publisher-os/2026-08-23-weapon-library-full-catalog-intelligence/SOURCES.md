# Source ledger

All external sources were observed 2026-08-23. First-party manufacturer and U.S. Government sources were preferred. A catalog's presence establishes product knowledge, not universal compatibility, issue status or authorization.

## SCZN3 baseline

- `docs/matrix.html` at commit `6846450752277eaad24e64b6d4a40578714fe85d` — current menu hierarchy, all local options, Glock/Canik structured records and sight/ammunition maps.
- `docs/architecture/SCZN3_SHORTEST_CORRECT_MENTAL_MODEL.md` — canonical separation of authority, knowledge and shooter presentation.
- Episode 56 catalog/coverage records — existing M4/M16/Navy target identities and capability boundaries.

## Firearm manufacturers

- [Colt current firearm navigation](https://www.colt.com/) — 1911, revolver and MSR families.
- [FN current pistols](https://fnamerica.com/products/pistols/) and [FN manuals](https://fnamerica.com/support/manuals/) — current pistol/rifle series and discontinued separation.
- [Daniel Defense 2023 catalog](https://danieldefense.com/media/wysiwyg/magalog/DD-Catalog-2023.pdf) — DDM4/DD5/DELTA 5 and configuration evidence; freshness caveat retained.
- [Ruger current firearms navigation](https://www.ruger.com/) and [Ruger product search](https://ruger.com/search/) — current lines and official almost-800-variations / 40-plus-lines scale statement.
- [SIG Sauer 2026 defense catalog](https://www.sigsauer.com/media/sigsauer/resources/FULL_DEFENSE_CAT_26_PRINT.pdf) — current weapon, optic and ammunition line census.
- [Springfield Armory compare/catalog navigation](https://www.springfield-armory.com/compare) — current rifle and handgun series.
- [RemArms rifle catalog](https://www.remarms.com/rifles/), [RemArms home](https://www.remarms.com/) and [current manuals](https://www.remarms.com/support/owners-manuals-%28remarms%29) — current families and firearm/ammunition company separation.
- [HK USA manuals](https://hk-usa.com/manuals/) — current/discontinued family evidence.
- [CZ-USA 2022 catalog](https://www.cz-usa.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/9996-CZ-2022-CatalogDIGITAL_2.10.22_R2.pdf) — useful family baseline; current-status confidence reduced due age.
- [GLOCK pistol taxonomy](https://us.glock.com/en/about/resources/all-about-pistols) — longslide/competition/standard/compact/subcompact/slimline/crossover taxonomy.
- [Canik catalog](https://www.canikarms.com/pdf/canik.pdf) — TP/METE family evidence; age caveat retained.
- [Walther 2024 catalog](https://waltherarms.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Walther-Product-Catalog-2024.pdf) — current/useful pistol families and variants.
- [Beretta current product families](https://www.beretta.com/en/product/) — current pistol, shotgun and rifle family navigation.
- [Mossberg current firearms](https://www.mossberg.com/firearms.html) — 309 product records overall and current family navigation.
- [Benelli current release/family evidence](https://www.benelliusa.com/resources/press-releases) — current 2026 shotgun family changes.
- [Winchester SXP](https://www.winchesterguns.com/products/shotguns/sxp.html) — exact current menu-relevant shotgun family.
- [Stoeger 2025 dealer workbook](https://stoegerindustries.com/sites/default/files/2025-03/2025-Stoeger%20Digital%20Workbook.pdf) — shotgun and pistol family index.

BCM, Aero Precision, Smith & Wesson and Savage were inventoried from the existing menu and current public manufacturer family structure, but their sites did not yield a stable first-party machine-readable denominator during this pass. Their census confidence and completeness are explicitly reduced rather than filled with estimates.

## Sighting-system manufacturers

- [Aimpoint Duty RDS/current series](https://www.aimpoint.com/duty-rds/) — exact series/product versus mount/accessory distinction.
- [EOTECH current manuals](https://www.eotechinc.com/pages/help-center-product-manuals) and [2025 catalog](https://www.eotechinc.com/media/documents/Catalog/EOTECH_2025_Catalog_MedRes.pdf) — HWS, magnifier/HHS and Vudu families.
- [Holosun product finder](https://www.holosun.com/product-finder) and [current series navigation](https://store.holosun.com/) — current pistol/rifle optic series, reticle and footprint evidence.
- [HK VP9 optic plate guide](https://hk-usa.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/VP9-LE-Brochure-7_29_25.pdf) — direct evidence that “optics ready” still requires exact footprint/plate compatibility.
- Trijicon, Vortex, Primary Arms and Leupold current families were cross-checked against their official product lines; exact SKU-scale ingestion remains a separate follow-up.

## U.S. military platform, optic and training authority

- [Army M4A1 Carbine](https://cpeground.army.mil/Equipment/Equipment-Portfolio/PM-MBCT-Lethality-Portfolio/M4A1-Carbine/) — exact platform characteristics and accessory relationships.
- [Army M16A4 Rifle](https://cpeground.army.mil/Equipment/Equipment-Portfolio/PM-MBCT-Lethality-Portfolio/M16A4-Rifle/) — exact M16A4 identity and replacement status.
- [Army M17/M18 Modular Handgun System](https://cpeground.army.mil/Equipment/Equipment-Portfolio/PM-MBCT-Lethality-Portfolio/M17-M18-Modular-Handgun-System/) — full-size/compact issued identities and specifications.
- [Army M500/M590 Shotgun](https://cpeground.army.mil/Equipment/Equipment-Portfolio/PM-MBCT-Lethality-Portfolio/M500-Shotgun/) — exact issued configurations.
- [Army M249 SAW](https://cpeground.army.mil/Equipment/Equipment-Portfolio/PM-MBCT-Lethality-Portfolio/M249-Squad-Automatic-Weapon/) — adjacent FN platform knowledge.
- [Army M68 CCO](https://www.peosoldier.army.mil/Equipment/Equipment-Portfolio/Project-Manager-Soldier-Lethality-Portfolio/M68-Close-Combat-Optic/) — role, 1x characteristics and M4/M16 relationship.
- [Army M150 RCO](https://cpeground.army.mil/Equipment/Equipment-Portfolio/PM-MBCT-Lethality-Portfolio/M150-Rifle-Combat-Optic/) — 4x, range-estimation and BDC role.
- [Army AN/PEQ-15/15A](https://www.peosoldier.army.mil/Equipment/Equipment-Portfolio/Project-Manager-Soldier-Maneuver-and-Precision-Targeting-Portfolio/Multifunctioning-Aiming-Light/) — visible/IR aiming and illumination relationship.
- [Army FWS-I](https://cpeground.army.mil/Equipment/Equipment-Portfolio/PM-MBCT-Lethality-Portfolio/Family-of-Weapon-Sights-Individual-FWS-I/) — M4-compatible thermal/in-line sighting relationship.
- [STP 21-1-SMCT, 16 Oct 2023](https://www.first.army.mil/Portals/102/STP%2021-1-SMCT.pdf) — M68, M150 and M4/M4A1 iron-sight adjustment evidence.
- [TC 3-20.40](https://rdl.train.army.mil/catalog-ws/view/100.ATSC%2FD6C17734-DD08-40FD-A50B-09448575646F-1564595433375/tc3_20x40wc1.pdf) — individual weapon training/qualification structure and range/target relationships.
- [Army Reserve Individual Weapons Training Strategy](https://www.usar.army.mil/Portals/98/Documents/Marksmanship/ARM_FY20-2.pdf?ver=2020-01-02-085219-327) — grouping, A8 zero, confirmation-at-distance and hold-off evidence.
- [M7/M250 type classification](https://cpeground.army.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4305601/project-manager-soldier-lethality-announces-type-classification-standard-tc-std/) — 2025 transition evidence; it does not imply completed replacement of M4A1/M249.
