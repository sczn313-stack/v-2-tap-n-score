## Sources deliberately not treated as sales truth

- Enterprise-wide revenue for Allen and GSM does not establish paper-target
  revenue.
- Marketplace analytics are modeled estimates for selected listings, not
  audited manufacturer sell-through and not additive across channels without a
  duplication adjustment.
- Government purchase examples establish a channel, not total government
  volume.
- Catalog breadth, package size, and printing capacity establish ability to
  sell volume, not demand by themselves.
- Publisher customer-count language is lifetime/marketing evidence unless an
  audited time period is supplied.
