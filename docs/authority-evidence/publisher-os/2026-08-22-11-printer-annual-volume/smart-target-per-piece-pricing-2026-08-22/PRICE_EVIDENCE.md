## Why a physical-sheet denominator matters

- “25 targets per sheet, 25-pack” is 25 physical sheets, not 625 fee units.
- A 432-count roll of separately marketed one-inch adhesive targets is 432
  physical target pieces.
- A target plus 110 repair pasters is one target for this model; the pasters do
  not receive separate Smart Target fees.
- A 1,000-pack sold for $32.50 is $0.0325 per physical target. A flat $0.10 fee
  would add $100 to the package, not $0.10 to the package.
