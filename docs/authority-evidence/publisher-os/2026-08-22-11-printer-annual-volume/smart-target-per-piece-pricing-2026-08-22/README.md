# P-OS Smart Target Per-Piece Pricing Study

Observation date: 2026-08-22

This study extends, rather than replaces, the parent 11-printer annual-volume
model. It evaluates fees for the Smart Target platform relationship. It does
not price printing, ink, the QR graphic, a label, or a physical target.

Files:

- `REPORT.md` — Founder conclusion, publisher economics, checkout examples,
  fee comparison, revenue tables, and recommendation.
- `MODEL.json` — machine-readable price summaries, volume cases, fee cases,
  penetrations, and recommendation.
- `PRICE_EVIDENCE.md` — package-to-piece observations and calculation notes.
- `SOURCES.md` — public and preserved-corpus source ledger.
- `validate_pricing_model.py` — deterministic revenue and fee-percentage checks.

The observed per-piece summaries are not sales-weighted medians unless a source
explicitly provides sales weights. “Representative” means an evidence-supported
product or central catalog observation suitable for fee sensitivity testing.
