## 3. What P-OS knows

P-OS now uses four separate layers:

1. **Governed target specification** — meaning owned by an organization or other valid authority, such as NRA B-16.
2. **Publisher target implementation identity** — one publisher's physical face/design lineage.
3. **Commercial target offering** — a sellable SKU, package, quantity, material, color, adhesive, size, or other configuration.
4. **Non-target commercial record** — an accessory, consumable, stand/hardware-only product, merchandise, or bundle that does not represent an individual shootable target identity.

> **Governed specification ≠ publisher implementation identity ≠ commercial offering.**

This audit classifies first, then counts. Quantity, color, paper stock, adhesive presentation, packaging, duplicate listings, and retailer evidence cannot manufacture a new target identity. Conversely, a single product page can contain several identities when it sells materially different faces or shapes.

### Why the original seven changed

- **Splatterburst:** 30 product identities expose **90** sellable quantity configurations; the earlier total counted only 30 page records as offerings.
- **Highwild:** 204 target pages collapse to **189** design lineages after pack/color reconciliation.
- **Birchwood Casey:** 53 target pages contain **74** identities and **90** offerings because named silhouette, 3D-stake, and target-kit options can be distinct physical target designs, while size/material options remain commercial variants.
- **EZ Aim / Allen:** 106 target product lineages represent 126 variant offerings; pack, size, and color variants do not create additional identities.
- **Champion:** 35 target pages contain **61** target-face/system lineages and 68 offerings; multi-face pages are expanded while package duplicates collapse.
- **Baker and GunFun:** their already-preserved 238- and 241-lineage analyses survive the new ontology; excluded multi-design packs remain separate commercial evidence.

Champion adds two exact current Grade-A catalog mappings—B-16 and B-3—and one historical/Grade-B B-2 lineage. Therefore:

No other organization label was promoted into a governed specification merely because a publisher printed NRA, IDPA, USPSA, IPSC, GSSF, CMP, IBS, military, law-enforcement, or agency language. Those records remain evidence links and future authority leads.

### Baker public rules

The preserved Baker rules establish publisher-owned instructions for Arcade, Battleship, Dartboard, Pinball, Candyville, and Primary. These close evidence gaps for six products but do not themselves activate capabilities.

Candyville directly establishes path/slide/color-transfer/reset behavior. That is evidence for one **publisher-defined path traversal candidate**. It is not authority to turn Collection/Hand into a path engine or to declare a universal game family from one lineage.

### Government and qualification products

Qualification-labeled products continue to support the existing architecture:

> **Versioned Qualification Profile → Qualification Executor → governed practice/simulation result.**

The printer establishes the product. The governing organization establishes the course. SCZN3 evaluation does not itself issue a government credential, instructor attestation, agency record, or official qualification.

## 8. Monitoring and downtime

All eleven are now represented in one Tier 3 structure. Future downtime work may compare, without onboarding another publisher:

- additions, removals, SKU changes, prices, availability, quantities, dimensions, material, and artwork;
- new or revised printed instructions, rules, manuals, and organization evidence;
- ownership, manufacturing/import, retailer, dealer, wholesale, advertising, media, sponsorship, and event signals;
- recognition evidence and authority gaps that close or conflict.

Historical evidence is append-only. A missing page is not proof of retirement. A changed fact records old value, new value, source, observation date, and evidence status.

Tier status means “reconciled under the common framework,” not “every desired fact exists publicly.” Tier 3 is **structure only**; no automatic monitoring service or cadence was implemented.
