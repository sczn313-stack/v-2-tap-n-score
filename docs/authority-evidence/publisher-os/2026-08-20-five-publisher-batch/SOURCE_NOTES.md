## Source hierarchy

Publisher offered-for-sale facts govern publisher/product identity. The normalization preserves source product IDs, exact exposed SKUs, names, commercial variants, dimensions found in titles/descriptions, images, descriptions, categories, availability, and prices. No third-party marketplace estimate or sales/ranking claim was used.

The Birchwood branded PDF is older than the live catalog. It is retained for provenance and supplemental printed-product context only. Current identity and availability come from the live publisher pages.
