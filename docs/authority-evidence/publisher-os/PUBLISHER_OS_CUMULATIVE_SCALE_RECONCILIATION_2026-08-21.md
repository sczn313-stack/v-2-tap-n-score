## Denominator reconciliation

The harmonized total is **1,451 publisher product records/pages**:

- 794 Baker/GunFun published product records: Baker 536 + GunFun 258.

This is the strongest comparable cumulative figure. A strict total of literal HTML product-detail pages opened is unavailable: the Baker work used 536 unique SKU records returned across six Store API pages, and the GunFun work used 258 published SKU records plus 22 shop listing pages. Those earlier API records must not be mislabeled as 794 browser page visits.

### Commercial offerings

The exact commercial-record total is **1,584**:

- Baker/GunFun: 794 unique published SKU records.
- Five-publisher batch: 790 variant-aware commercial offering records.

The 1,584 records reconcile exactly as:

> 1,168 target offerings + 302 accessories/consumables/non-target offerings + 114 separately preserved older-format pack/bundle exclusions = 1,584.

The 114 pack/bundle records are not called accessories. They consist of 111 Baker variety/Get-Ten packaging records and three GunFun multi-design Splatter Packs that the earlier reports deliberately excluded from their individual-target denominator.
