## Architecture test

The proposed four-layer model accurately describes the NRA paper ecosystem, with two cautions:

1. A manufacturer sheet connects the organization specification to a licensed manufacturer, while the live catalog connects that manufacturer to a commercial product/package. Both links must be preserved.
2. Course/qualification meaning may live in a versioned NRA rulebook or program outside the target specification. One physical specification may support multiple independently resolved mission families; multi-capability never means multi-authority execution.

Therefore one authority-complete NRA target profile can truthfully drive the same SCZN3 scoring or practice/simulation meaning across multiple licensed publisher implementations, provided each commercial product is reliably bound to that exact specification and retains its publisher identity. It cannot silently confer current sanctioned-print status, official qualification issuance, or commercial identity across printers.

## Business significance

The first seven publishers proved that product scale collapses into reusable shooter behaviors. These four add a second kind of leverage: **many competing commercial products can implement the same externally governed paper specification**.

At minimum, the 105 NRA specifications offered by multiple current printers are direct candidates for authority reuse. The 54 offered by all four show especially broad leverage: package, price, printer, and purchase path vary, while the governed target meaning need not be rebuilt. This gives P-OS a comparison layer it did not previously possess—availability and commercial choice across implementations of the same authoritative target.

The market lesson is:

> Learn the printer. Learn the paper. Learn who defines what the paper means. Preserve all three separately.

## Commercial intelligence limitations

Bounded public search did not reliably establish major-retailer breadth, wholesale terms, advertising spend, sponsorships, or owned social/YouTube channels for all four. Those fields remain unknown rather than being filled with search-result proximity or namesake noise.
