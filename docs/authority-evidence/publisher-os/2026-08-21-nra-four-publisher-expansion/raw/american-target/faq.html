FAQ

DISCOUNTS

What type of discounts do you offer?
We do have discounts for any orders over 5000 total targets. Call or e-mail us to get a quote.

Discounts are applicable only if paid within 30 days ... NET THEREAFTER

PAYMENT

What type of payment do you accept?
We take Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. For customers who have established credit, our terms are Net 30 days. We also accept purchase order numbers.

SHIPPING

Shipments will be made in most cases within 48 hours after receipt of your order. They will be shipped the fastest and most economical method. COD's, UPS AIR, Motor Freight COD, Special Handling, Air Freight, Air Parcel Post will be rated for any additional costs and you will be billed extra.

You say you pay the "bulk" of the shipping charges, what does that mean?
UPS has fees for residential deliveries, $2.20 per package, as well as a delivery area surcharge, $1.85 per package, for rural areas. Also, any expedited, special handling or COD's are added to the cost. Once we get your shipping information, we will let you know which, if any, of these fees apply.

What about large orders shipped via truck line, are there added charges?
There are fees which apply for residential deliveries, expedited service or any special handling. For all standard, business to business deliveries, we pay the shipping costs.

What about shipments outside the contiguous 48 states?
Extra fees are involved for orders to Hawaii and Alaska as well as international shipments. Please call or e mail us the information and we will quote you a price.

Is expedited service available?
Yes, please provide us with the details and we will quote it for you.

QUANTITIES and PRICES

Targets are listed in minimum package amount and will not be sold in lesser quantities. Targets are priced per Package or 1M quantities. Purchases of less than 1M are subject to the Package price in multiples up to the 1M quantities. All purchases of 1M and over in multiples of package quantity are subject to the 1M price.

Target Pricing
