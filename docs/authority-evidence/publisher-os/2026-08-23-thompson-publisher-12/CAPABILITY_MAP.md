# Thompson Target Inc. — SCZN3 Capability Map

Observed: 2026-08-23  
Status: additive Publisher #12 analysis; no production registration

## Result

**Thompson requires zero new behavioral capability families.** Its catalog adds
new publisher products, compositions, and authority-packaging work—not a new
kind of shooter behavior.

Every one of the 109 target identities can inherit `smartEvidenceCapture` once
registered. Richer experiences remain independently governed by exact product,
region, rule, course, and mission authority.

Family counts below are non-exclusive because one physical product may expose
multiple independently selected governed experiences.

## Strongest inherited-capability lineages

1. **TAC Target, R-2704 variants** — directly fits Cue/Drill. Thompson publishes
   that a partner cues one of four torsos and describes Time Factor Scoring.
   Full execution still requires an exact governed transcription of the Cue
   Card and scoring formula/profile; the application owner already exists.
2. **Trouble-Shooter and Group-Shooter families** — directly fit Diagnostic
   Feedback. Registration plus exact publisher region/label profiles can
   produce publisher-label matching without SCZN3 asserting causes or coaching.
3. **HALO Threat Code: RED, R-4655 variants** — fits Recreational Challenge and
   can expose a separately selected Decision experience. Thompson publishes a
   10-round, point/penalty/bonus game, but exact region geometry still must be
   packaged as governed data.
4. **B27Q red/blue full- and half-size products** — fit Smart Evidence now and
   Qualification practice/simulation when an external authoritative Course
   Profile exists. Thompson's product identity does not establish a current
   government or agency course.
5. **Progressive Pistol Training 60 Pack** — a publisher-established composition
   of Group-Shooter, smaller practice targets, and TAC. Existing Diagnostic,
   Cue/Drill, scoring, and SEC Progression owners can represent the pieces; a
   governed program profile and backend subject continuity are required for a
   longitudinal claim.
6. **Scope, M-Rad, Dead Center, Sight-Seer and related families** — inherit
   existing Zeroing Authority once exact geometry, scale, units, and intended
   setup are packaged.

## Authority gaps, not architecture failures

- The four B27Q identities do not bring an official course of fire with them.
- Thompson's `75% IPSC/USPSA` product page establishes publisher labeling and a
  publisher claim of conformance. The reviewed public evidence does not, by
  itself, establish an official USPSA relationship or scoring license.
- TAC's page describes the behavior, but the included Cue Card is the best
  candidate authority for exact cue/sequence/timing/profile packaging.
- Trouble-Shooter pages contain causal/corrective publisher language. V1 SCZN3
  may match observations to exact publisher labels only; causal advice remains
  unavailable unless separately authorized.
- Threat Code: RED publishes rules in text, but exact target regions and all four
  scenarios must reconcile before the richer experience can execute.
- 3D Hostage and 3D Werewolf create visual-recognition and presentation work,
  not a seventeenth capability family.

## Scale conclusion

Thompson supports the Episode 56 rule:

> Publisher products inherit capabilities; they do not become individual
> software implementations.

No target-ID/SKU production branch is proposed. Target-specific work that
legitimately remains is identity, images, dimensions, UGO, exact publisher
rules/labels, external course authority, and governed contract/profile data.
