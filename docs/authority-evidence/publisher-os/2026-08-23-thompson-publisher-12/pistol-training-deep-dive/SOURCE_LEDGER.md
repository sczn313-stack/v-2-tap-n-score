## Evidence boundaries

- Public access demonstrates existence and publisher ownership/provenance; it
  is not assumed to grant production reproduction, voice or derivative-use
  rights.
- Printed causal/corrective statements remain Thompson's statements. P-OS does
  not validate their technical correctness independently.
- Product photographs are sufficient for analysis and recognition evidence;
  production UGO requires exact publisher geometry/vector authority.
- The Progressive Pistol component order is established. Advancement criteria
  are not.
- TAC timing is established as a rule, but SCZN3 must separately establish the
  provenance of the elapsed-time evidence.
- Threat Code game rules are established; panel order and pistol-specific use
  are not.
