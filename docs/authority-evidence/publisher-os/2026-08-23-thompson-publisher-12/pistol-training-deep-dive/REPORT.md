# P-OS — Thompson Pistol-Training Authority Deep Dive

Observed: 2026-08-23  
Status: research and authority analysis only; no production registration or implementation

## Founder answer

**Yes—Thompson contains a real publisher-authored pistol tutoring system.** It
is not one generic “pistol target” and it is not authority-complete as a single
unit. It resolves into three already-known SCZN3 behaviors:

1. **Progression:** Group-Shooter → Trouble-Shooter → TAC.
2. **Diagnosis:** six pistol diagnostic identities carrying Thompson's printed
   patterns, fundamentals and corrective content.
3. **Guided drill/scenario:** TAC's published Cue Card and Threat Code: RED's
   published game rules.

The content is sufficiently concrete to justify a Thompson authority/licensing
conversation and a Founder Specification. It does **not** authorize production
reuse merely because it is publicly visible.

The architectural finding is equally important:

> Thompson requires zero new behavioral families. It exposes missing authority
> packaging and one deliberate Diagnostic V1 scope boundary—not missing target
> software.

## What Thompson actually teaches

### 1. Progressive Pistol Training — R-6502

Thompson establishes an exact ordinal progression and supplies 20 of each
component:

1. **Group-Shooter Handgun, R-9200** — fundamentals plus feedback based on group
   location.
2. **Trouble-Shooter Handgun 19 x 25, R-2550** — four smaller aim areas and
   printed reminders/diagrams for misplaced groups.
3. **TAC, R-2704** — partner/solo audible-cue work and an optional Time Factor
   result.

The component identities are confirmed by Thompson's product description and
bundle image. Thompson does **not** publish a score threshold, number of
sessions, required group size, pass/fail rule, or other performance criterion
that says when the shooter is ready to advance.

Therefore SCZN3 may truthfully say:

> “The next stage in Thompson's published sequence is Trouble-Shooter.”

It may not yet say:

> “Your result proves you are ready to advance.”

That richer claim needs Thompson-authored advancement rules. Cross-session
progression additionally needs the already-identified governed backend subject
continuity owner.

### 2. Group-Shooter Handgun — R-9200 variants

The target establishes a sector method: locate a shot or group, follow that
sector to the edge, and consult Thompson's paired problem/correction. It prints
distinct right- and left-handed treatment and handgun fundamentals.

Visible publisher pattern labels include jumping the gun, heel, post-trigger
release, lazy finger, all thumbs, jerking the trigger, over-gripping, and wrist
break directions. The printed fundamentals cover position, grip, breath
control, sight alignment, trigger squeeze, and follow-through.

This is stronger than generic marketing—it is structured publisher knowledge.
But the current Diagnostic Feedback V1 is deliberately limited to exact
publisher-region/label matching. It forbids SCZN3 from selecting a dominant
group diagnosis or emitting corrective advice.

### 3. Trouble-Shooter family

| Product | Base SKU | Printed structure | After-shot meaning established |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trouble-Shooter 19 x 25 | R-2550 | Four 8-inch aim areas; 1-inch grid; eight comparison diagrams; right/left guidance | Shooter compares a group with one of eight publisher examples, then consults Thompson's paired guidance. |
| Trouble-Shooter 12.5 x 19 | R-2551-10 | Four 6-inch aim areas; 1-inch grid; same eight-pattern structure | Same diagnostic method in different geometry. |
| Trouble-Shooter 9 x 12 | R-2552-12 | One aim area; 1-inch grid; printed fundamentals | Fundamentals reference, not an automatic eight-pattern diagnosis. |
| HALO Trouble-Shooter 8.5 x 11 | R-4613 | One reactive aim area; eight-pattern chart; right/left guidance | Same publisher comparison concept; reactive marks improve visibility only. |
| HALO Trouble-Shooter 12.5 x 19 | R-4646 | Five reactive aim areas; eight-pattern chart; right/left guidance | Same diagnostic concept with more practice areas. |

The recurring publisher labels are: the jerk, jumping the gun, all thumbs, the
heel, lobster grip, lazy finger, wrist breaker, and green-horn. Thompson pairs
these labels with causal/corrective statements.

Public evidence establishes that Thompson owns and publishes this content. It
does not by itself supply a production license, machine-readable group-match
criteria, confidence/ambiguity rules, or permission for SCZN3 to reproduce and
voice the corrective text.

### 4. TAC — R-2704 variants

TAC is the strongest authority result. Thompson publicly supplies the Cue Card,
which establishes:

- target designed for at least a shooter and partner/caller;
- 10–20 recommended shots;
- pistol distance of 3–5 yards and rifle distance of 5–10 yards;
- low-ready start and return to low ready after every shot;
- partner calls one of four torsos and operates a stopwatch-capable timer;
- 10 publisher cue sets, each containing 20 cues;
- cue vocabulary by color, shape, or phonetic letter;
- target scoring zones of 5, 3 and 1;
- timer starts with the first cue and stops after the final shot;
- Time Factor equals total target score divided by elapsed seconds;
- firearm is secured before scoring; then the next shooter can repeat.

This maps directly to the existing Cue/Drill Orchestrator and precision-zone
evaluation. SCZN3 can own cue selection, sequence, event state, target UGO,
score calculation, SEC and preservation through profile/contract data.

The remaining truth boundary is timing. Manual shot observations cannot become
physical shot-time evidence. Time Factor is available only when elapsed time is
provided by an admitted timer/clock relationship that actually covers the
publisher's first-cue-to-final-shot interval. Without that, SCZN3 can preserve
the drill and score while marking Time Factor unavailable.

### 5. HALO Threat Code: RED — R-4655 variants

Thompson publishes a complete top-level game rule:

- one or more players;
- 10 rounds per target;
- red threat zones add their printed values;
- black innocent/negative zones subtract their printed values;
- 20-point bonus for scoring on all threats;
- highest total wins;
- four illustrated scenario panels;
- optional increased distance or timer for added difficulty.

The target image exposes the printed positive and negative values. Existing
Recreational Challenge can calculate the game once exact UGO is packaged. A
Decision experience must remain separately selected and separately governed;
the artwork cannot become a legal/tactical/threat judgment.

One authority gap remains: the public wording does not clearly establish a
required order among the four panels. SCZN3 must not invent a panel sequence.
The reviewed page also does not establish pistol-specific applicability, so it
is a guided shooting-game candidate rather than a claimed pistol curriculum
stage.

### 6. Premium Handgun 120 Pack — R-1301

This offering contains 20 each of six named targets: Group-Shooter, Sight-Seer
Red, Trouble-Shooter, Center-Fire Life Size Silhouette, B27-IMZ Upper Torso, and
HALO Life Size Silhouette.

It is a curated handgun collection, not a progression. SCZN3 may expose the
authorized experience for each registered component. It may not create an
ordered course or advancement claim for the bundle.

## Smallest correct reusable extension

No seventh behavioral family is indicated. The smallest missing reusable piece
is a controlled extension to the existing Diagnostic Feedback authority:

> **Publisher-Authored Guidance Presentation — Not an SCZN3 Diagnosis**

The extension would require:

1. Licensed, versioned publisher guidance records—not arbitrary display text.
2. Exact target/profile/handedness and UGO binding.
3. A governed group-pattern matcher with explicit minimum evidence,
   confidence, ambiguity and unmatched states; no forced result.
4. Output that preserves the difference between:
   - observed evidence;
   - SCZN3's governed match;
   - Thompson's label;
   - Thompson's guidance.
5. Exact source/version provenance in the SEC.
6. No paraphrased SCZN3 coaching, independent causal conclusion, medical,
   anatomical, tactical or legal claim.
7. A content/license owner controlling whether Thompson text may be displayed,
   spoken, cached and preserved.

This is a genuine Founder specification/approval boundary because Gear 3 was
explicitly locked against causal diagnosis and corrective advice. Public
publisher evidence proves the opportunity; it does not silently amend that
boundary.

## Best product proof sequence

If Thompson supplies/authorizes the exact assets, the lowest-risk proof order is:

1. **TAC** — strongest authority, existing Cue/Drill owner, concrete complete
   flow, high Listen/Try value.
2. **Trouble-Shooter 12.5 x 19** — validates group evidence, handedness,
   ambiguity and licensed publisher guidance without target-specific code.
3. **Progressive Pistol** — composes the two proofs with Group-Shooter and tests
   publisher-defined “next stage” without inventing readiness criteria.
4. **Threat Code RED** — validates exact points/penalties/bonus and independent
   recreational/decision mission selection.

## Final answer

Thompson did hand P-OS something important for pistol shooters:

> A publisher-authored sequence that can tell the shooter what target comes
> next; diagnostic targets that carry Thompson's own after-shot meaning; and a
> Cue Card detailed enough for the target to talk a shooter through a governed
> drill.

The immediate opportunity is **not** “let AI coach pistol shooting.” It is:

> **Observe evidence → resolve exact Thompson authority → present Thompson's
> authorized meaning → preserve what was actually established.**

That can make Pistol Skills substantially smarter while keeping Thompson as the
instruction author and SCZN3 as the governed digital relationship.
