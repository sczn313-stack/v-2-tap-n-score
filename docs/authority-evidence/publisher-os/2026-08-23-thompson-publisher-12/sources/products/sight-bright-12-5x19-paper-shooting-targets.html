    SIGHT-BRIGHT 12.5"x19" Paper Bullseye Shooting Targets

    • Ultra bright fluorescent yellow and red for optimum visibility
    • See every shot more clearly even from long distance
    • Printed on high quality thick paper
    • Made in the U.S.A.

    Ultra bright target plus dark bullet hole equals superior contrast for the easiest down range visibility. Thompson's new SIGHT-BRIGHT is a twist on the classic bullseye target with bright neon-red 4" bulls on a fluorescent yellow background. The extreme color contrast results in a brighter target with better sight-to-target alignment, making it the optimum visual for all pistol and rifle shooting applications. A scoring area is included on each target.

    Designed for handguns and rifles at distances of 25 to 100 yards.
