## Founder summary

### WHO THOMPSON IS

**Thompson Target Inc.** is the target publisher/manufacturer at
[thompsontarget.com](https://www.thompsontarget.com/), not Thompson/Center Arms.

### KEYS TO THE VAULT

**TAC is the strongest single-target Key.** A shooter can listen to the
publisher-established behavior, try the cue-driven experience, preserve a
truthful result, and then return to Thompson for the physical target.

**Progressive Pistol is the strongest program Key.** It can connect multiple
publisher products to multiple existing capability owners while keeping every
mission family and SEC independently governed.

**Trouble-Shooter is the strongest repeat-use Key.** It provides a natural
Listen/Try/Buy entry into an eight-identity diagnostic lineage and a contextual
publisher-discovery path, without calling publisher labels an SCZN3 diagnosis.

## Program and organization findings

- **TAC** is a genuine publisher program/system and fits Cue/Drill. Its
  publisher page describes partner cues, four torsos, low-ready operation and a
  score/time concept. Exact execution still requires the authoritative Cue Card
  and governed timing/scoring profile.
- **Progressive Pistol** is a genuine stepped publisher composition, not merely
  marketing copy: 20 each of three target types, beginning with Group-Shooter,
  moving to smaller practice targets, and ending with TAC. Longitudinal claims
  still require backend subject continuity and an exact program profile.
- **Threat Code: RED** publishes enough textual game rules to be a high-priority
  authority-packaging candidate: 10 rounds, positive and negative areas, a
  bonus, highest score wins, four scenarios. Exact region authority is still
  required.
- **B27Q / qualification** is a product relationship, not a complete official
  qualification. The government/agency owning a course remains the course
  authority; instructors, facilities, witnessing, reporting and credentials
  remain separate official-claim owners.
- **75% IPSC/USPSA** establishes Thompson's label and conformance claim. The
  public organization evidence reviewed did not establish endorsement,
  licensing, or official target status. P-OS preserves that as an authority gap.
- **SIG SAUER** is a confirmed product collaborator and distribution/business
  relationship, not a shooting-rule governing body.

## What did not become a new capability

- HALO reactive printing changes physical feedback, not digital behavior.
- Zombies, aliens, werewolves, hostage art and humorous targets are themes.
- 3D anaglyph presentation is a recognition/presentation trait.
- Rifle, pistol and shotgun applicability changes profiles and evidence, not
  owner architecture.
- Multiple sizes, colors, materials, package quantities and UPC/SKU variants are
  commercial data.

## Founder conclusion

Publisher #12 strengthens—not weakens—the Episode 56 architecture claim:

> We don't build software for every target. We build software for what shooters
> do. Thompson products inherit those abilities when their authoritative
> information says they can.

Thompson's most interesting contribution is not another 109 targets. It is the
way one publisher combines targets, cue cards, diagnostic labels, game rules,
program progression, retailer/dealer distribution, and a major-brand
collaboration. The existing engine can express all of it through governed data.
